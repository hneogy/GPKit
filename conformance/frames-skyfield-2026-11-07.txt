The grid is every ten minutes for six hours from each set's epoch cut to the minute. GPKit takes UT1 to be UTC, and
Skyfield's builtin table puts UT1 less UTC at up to 0.24 s on these dates; the lines "with UT1" turn the Earth by
Skyfield's value through a door the tests have, so they compare the frames themselves. The right ascension is for the
true equator and equinox of the date (Skyfield's radec(epoch="date")), geometric, from the place 100 m up.
The ground track is the point beneath at each step, and agrees with Skyfield's point beneath in
Tests/GPKitTests/Resources/skyfield-passes.json as conformance/skyfield-2026-10-07.txt says. The footprint has no
outside reference: each place on it is held to GPKit's look, which is checked against Skyfield, and to its bearing.
