Added 2026-11-07: "identical to the bit" above holds for the same build mode on the same system: the port and the
C++ were compiled alike (debug builds) and run on one machine, each run above on its own. An optimised build, or
another system's C maths library, can round a sine or a cosine differently in the last bit: on macOS an optimised
build calls __sincos_stret where a sine and a cosine of one angle meet, whose sine differed from sin's for 392 of
1,367,990 arguments tried, and glibc's sin and cos differ from macOS's. Measured on GPKit's pass-check sets
(python/README.md, GPKit #20), that moved positions by at most 1.8e-12 km between a debug and a release build on
macOS, and by at most 7.3e-12 km between macOS and Linux release builds. The agreement the tests require, within a
micrometre, is untouched.
