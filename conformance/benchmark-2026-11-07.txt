GPKit's propagation, one element set at a time and in its batch (Propagator.states), on 10,000 element sets.
Run on 2026-11-07 (UTC), 05:40Z, on a Mac17,4 (Apple M5, 10 cores: 4 performance, 6 efficiency, 16 GB), macOS 27.2
(26B123), Apple Swift version 6.4.2, release build, at GPKit main 5348d48 with the batch added.

How it was run, the same on any machine:

  swift run -c release --package-path tools/benchmark benchmark

The element sets are made up: 10,000 of them by a rule in tools/benchmark, in four shells like a large
constellation's (about 550 km at 53 degrees, 540 at 53.2, 570 at 70 and 560 at 97.6), the same on every run. No
provider's data is in them. Each figure is the median of 5 runs after one to warm up. Before anything is timed the
tool checks that the batch's results are the one-at-a-time results, to the bit.
