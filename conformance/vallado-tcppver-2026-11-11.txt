  every number, and the same numbers off, in every column group.

  27 lines of set 20413's run 3.5 years out, where a difference in the last bits of the trigonometric functions grows
  to 1.17e-7 km (within gpconf's bar of 2e-7), and two numbers that sit on a rounding edge (28057 and 28129, about

  2.45 million days in one double, 1 microsecond off in 6 lines and, for 23333 at 720 minutes, 23:59:59.999155
  printed where the sum here gives the next midnight. No column of it depends on GPKit's propagation.
- The elements: all 634 lines printed alike.
- So "line for line within its printed digits" holds for GPKit exactly as far as it holds for Vallado's own code on
  this platform; the lines beyond it are the file's platform, which tcppver.out does not name [inferred: the
  cause, from the C++ here giving the same numbers as GPKit; the platform the file was made on is not known].
